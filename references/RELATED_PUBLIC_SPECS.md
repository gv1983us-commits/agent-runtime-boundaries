## Scope relationship

### BEC

BEC owns task-scoped execution-evidence acceptance. ARB explains why reasoning, visible status, or delivery cannot substitute for invocation evidence or a BEC result.

### MPAA

MPAA owns portable agent architecture and Runtime Report semantics. ARB supplies non-normative functional distinctions and does not amend the six-document authority matrix.

### PCA

PCA owns bounded process-continuity assessment. ARB distinguishes storage, retrieval, working state, commitment, and continuation without issuing PCA status.

### Review Protocol

The Review Protocol owns exact-source and review-receipt discipline. ARB consumes that discipline for source receipts but does not import review validity as analytical truth.

### CDTS

CDTS owns cross-domain correlation traces. ARB may be cited as context but cannot be declared a normative owner or trace verdict source.

## No transitive canonical status

Publication or canonization of ARB does not make it canonical for BEC, MPAA, PCA, the Review Protocol, or CDTS.
